# Review Motion And Qualify Visual Source

This topic carries one bounded step of the Playthings visual-source production process.

## Current Read

For gait review, the ordered motion contact is primary because all eight poses are simultaneously judgeable and expose foot placement, pose progression, repetition, and weak variation. An animated GIF or equivalent temporal preview is secondary evidence for cadence, loop continuity, and obvious temporal pops.

A video/GIF alone is insufficient when it obscures the pose pattern visible in the ordered contact. Mechanical extraction PASS does not imply that gait is perceptually good.

## Design Direction

Sigma or another explicit human acceptance authority reviews the ordered contact first and temporal preview second. Rejection returns the whole visual-source candidate to generation; the process does not locally redesign a rejected gait frame-by-frame.

On human acceptance, the frozen extracted source may feed downstream schema/artifact sheet-family and LOD/export design. Acceptance of this process or one source bundle must not be treated as acceptance of still-open world grid, interior, fullscreen, time, action vocabulary, resolution, or final sheet-layout decisions.
