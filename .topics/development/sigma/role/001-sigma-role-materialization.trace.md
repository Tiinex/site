# Sigma role materialization

## Objective

Materialize the first current durable `Sigma` Role under current Party Role authority so fresh Tiinex collaborators can distinguish the human structural capacity from the `Q` conversational handle, from transport/session posture, and from the historical experimental Sigma definition in `Tiinex/ai`.

## Done Criteria

- A current `tiinex.party.role.v1` Sigma artifact exists with explicit scope, authority, holder, and interpretation boundaries.
- Sigma remains a peer capacity rather than a manager, hidden steering layer, universal product authority, or technical fallback role.
- The useful historical structural function survives as evidence-informed continuity without importing the legacy `Tiinex/ai` role as current authority.
- Current observed collaboration semantics are explicit enough for fresh roles to understand that Sigma input is feedback/signal by default, may include deliberate framing or bias probes, and does not decide another role's grounding or operational readiness.
- Human product/host observation, prioritization, and gap-filling remain representable without turning ordinary Sigma statements into canon, technical proof, or automatic acceptance.
- Courier/transport behavior remains outside Sigma Role semantics.

## Scope

Sigma role-capacity materialization only. No permanent holder assignment, Party identity creation, schema-semantic change, project hierarchy, product acceptance grant, technical implementation ownership, or transport Role is implied. Future clarification should continue through durable Role lineage/evidence rather than by treating conversation memory as the missing part of this artifact.
