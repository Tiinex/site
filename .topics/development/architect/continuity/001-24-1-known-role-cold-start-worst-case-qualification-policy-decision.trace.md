# Known Role cold-start worst-case qualification policy decision

The current trust tranche is intentionally optimized for the hardest ordinary consumer state rather than for preserving convenience from warm conversation history.

## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: evidence standard and retry discipline for `001-24-known-role-cold-start-trust-closure-task.trace.md`
- Decision: during the current known-Role trust tranche, fresh-project/fresh-conversation execution is the qualifying path whenever feasible. A failure discovered in one Role session is corrected through durable artifacts and normal Handoff routing, but that same coached session cannot qualify the corrected behavior. A later fresh Role must demonstrate the current contract without Q re-teaching it.
- Qualification Unit: record Anchor, Axiom, Loom and Kodax separately. One Role's success does not imply sibling qualification, and one implementation author's successful return does not self-accept the implementation or the Role routine.
- Human Return Boundary: structured Tooling output may serve as machine/review evidence, but normal human completion remains one primary Handoff package plus one adjacent Tooling-derived minimal routing block. A cold Role exposing additional internal evidence because instructions are ambiguous is a contract finding, not a reason to lower the gate.

## Basis

- Q explicitly prefers worst-case cold-start evidence because a routine that reliably works without prior conversation state should not become harder in a medium/warm continuation.
- Repeated Loom runs showed that correct package mechanics and focused regressions can coexist with final-meter human transport drift; same-session correction therefore measures coachability, not durable system reliability.
- Tooling 018 moved normal inline routing into the ordinary manufacture result, but the fresh Loom 008-2 return exposed a remaining distinction between exact structured completion evidence and the intentionally minimal human transport surface.

## Consequences

- Active Anchor should independently review the Loom 008-2 Tooling 018 return and classify the new machine-evidence-versus-human-emission finding before choosing the next fresh Role qualification route.
- No same-session Loom correction is requested by this decision. The next qualifying evidence comes from another fresh consumer after any required contract correction has been routed and returned.
- Existing source, performance, context-audit and Role-materialization gaps remain separate and fail closed; cold-start rigor does not erase them.
- The zero-state/bootstrap-only starter-discovery scenario is preserved as a later trust gate after the known-Role tranche rather than expanding the current task mid-flight.

## Review Conditions

Revisit this policy only if the host makes fresh-project isolation impossible to distinguish, or if repeated evidence shows that the qualification setup itself introduces a non-representative failure mode. Warm-context success may supplement but not replace the current cold-start evidence while this decision remains active.
