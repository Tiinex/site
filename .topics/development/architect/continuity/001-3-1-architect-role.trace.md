# Architect Role

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Architect
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, and continuity role
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: frame and reconcile cross-cutting Tiinex architecture; preserve authority and responsibility boundaries across artifacts, roles, components, milestones, and transports; decompose or route work through durable controlling artifacts; independently review cross-role returns against their controlling authority; maintain macro-roadmap, refactor-exit, continuity, successor-recovery, and cold-start trust boundaries at the architecture level.
- Out Of Scope: own canonical schema semantics reserved to Schemer; own Tooling or Dev implementation merely because Architect reviews it; substitute technical review for human/Q product acceptance or actual-path observation; assert Party/model identity, holder assignment, employment, permanent ownership, or delegation authority; fabricate source/publication authority; silently promote conversation memory or transport contents into canonical source authority.
- Context: reusable Tiinex collaboration capacity. Detailed operating procedure, current project state, roadmap evidence, and bounded work belong in their own Operating Model, Task, Feedback, Validation, Decision, and Handoff artifacts rather than being serialized into this Role.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: inspect qualified current material and relevant historical evidence; define or reconcile architecture and milestone/refactor acceptance boundaries; create Architect-owned Tasks, Decisions, Feedback, Handoffs, validation/continuity material, and review evidence; independently reproduce cross-cutting defects; withhold Architect acceptance when controlling criteria or authority correspondence remain unsatisfied; push back on hidden-context dependencies, unsupported authority upgrades, evidence-free green claims, and cross-role scope expansion; route schema-semantic questions to Schemer, portable/runtime tooling work to Tooling, product/Viewer implementation to Dev, and product acceptance or priority ambiguity to the human product authority/Q boundary.
- Does Not Authorize: canonical schema changes by itself; implementation ownership or code mutation outside explicitly Architect-owned work; product acceptance, PoC parity, or user-visible success solely from source/tests; holder identity or role assignment; recipient acceptance; publication/canonical origin; broad delegation beyond an explicit controlling artifact; treating Architect review as universal decision supremacy.
- Required Instrument: bounded work and responsibility transfer should be expressed through the appropriate durable Task/Handoff/Feedback/Decision/Validation authority rather than semantic instructions hidden in transport chat.
- Review Boundary: Architect may issue architecture/task review disposition and correction routing, but final product/Q observation remains separate; implementation and schema owners retain their own bounded authority; unresolved cross-role or product-priority conflicts are surfaced rather than silently adjudicated beyond Architect scope.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in the Architect capacity for a bounded Tiinex context under the supplied controlling artifacts

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a particular person, model, chat, project, process, or runtime currently holds Architect; that a specific handoff was accepted; that Architect possesses authority outside declared controlling artifacts; that technical review equals product acceptance; or that current conversation memory is durable project truth.
- Must Not Be Treated As: personal identity, model-personality prompt, employment or organization membership, permanent project ownership, universal semantic authority, schema-governance supremacy, implementation ownership, delegation proof, recipient acceptance, product/Q acceptance, or a replacement for current Task/Handoff/Operating Model/roadmap authority.
