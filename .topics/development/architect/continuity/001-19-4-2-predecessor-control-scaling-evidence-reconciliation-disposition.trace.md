- [The predecessor-control contradiction signal](001-19-4-predecessor-control-roundtrip-scaling-contradiction-signal.trace.md) adds a greater-than-180-second bounded observation on the returned Loom workspace with `--no-roundtrip`. That workspace was related but not byte-identical to Loom's incoming control state, so the observation contradicts any broad current-non-reproduction summary without invalidating the fast measurements.
