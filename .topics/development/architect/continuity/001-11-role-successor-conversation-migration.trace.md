# Role successor conversation migration

## Objective

Move the remaining active AI collaboration roles from their long-lived older-project conversations to fresh successor conversations in the current Tiinex project, proving that each can recover its bounded role, authority limits, current work, and return behavior from durable artifacts and Handoff packages rather than hidden predecessor chat state.

## Done Criteria

- Loom, Axiom, and Kodax each receive a fresh successor conversation with no requirement to continue the old chat history.
- Each successor starts from current workspace/package authority plus exact durable Role/identity/Handoff artifacts; predecessor conversation content is not copied wholesale into the new operational context.
- Each role independently recovers its lane, peer/no-hierarchy boundary, pushback rights, current Task/Handoff scope, and evidence/return obligations without manual semantic rescue hidden in transport prose.
- Each role performs at least one real bounded work/review leaf appropriate to its lane and returns durable evidence sufficient for independent review.
- Role-specific successor `tiinex.party.role.v1` artifacts under Loom/Axiom/Kodax may be materialized when the role migration is mature; until then the accepted predecessor Role semantics plus current name mapping remain explicit rather than replaced by labels alone.
- Qualification distinguishes fresh-session success from repeatability, cross-runtime robustness, product acceptance, or permanent trust; no stronger trust level is inferred from one successful successor run.
- Old role conversations may remain readable for deliberate archaeology but are no longer required to answer "what are we doing?", recover role boundaries, locate current authority, or continue normal work.
- Human transport may dogfood the planned transport-only participation posture so the human can carry exact package/routing material without becoming a technical intermediary.
- Migration order may be Loom, then Axiom, then Kodax to keep one bounded successor lane active at a time; this sequencing is operational convenience and creates no hierarchy among the roles.

## Scope

This task retires conversation dependence, not historical conversational evidence. Do not delete/archive old chats merely to claim closure if their current product UI still makes them useful as historical records.

Do not infer that a fresh conversation holds a Role from chat title, model name, account display name, project membership, or package receipt. Role/capacity must be established by explicit current artifacts/routing and bounded participation.

Sigma's revised durable human Role definition remains separate work. Anchor has already demonstrated qualification-once in a fresh successor environment; this task uses that method as evidence, not as automatic qualification of the other peer roles.

## Dependencies

- [Role family identity transition decision](001-8-1-role-family-identity-transition-decision.trace.md) for current labels and peer/no-hierarchy authority.
- [First true Architect cold-start qualification](001-7-first-true-architect-cold-start-qualification.trace.md), its method/evidence lineage, and [qualification-once decision](001-7-3-architect-cold-start-qualification-once-decision.trace.md) as the demonstrated predecessor-conversation-independence pattern.
- Current durable predecessor Role artifacts: Architect/Anchor, Tooling/Loom, published Schemer/Axiom, and Dev/Kodax boundaries as preserved by the identity-transition decision.
- Current recipient-relative Handoff package/material-closure foundation so each successor can receive complete explicit material without making transport text carry the work semantics.
