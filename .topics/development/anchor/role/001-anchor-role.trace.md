# Anchor Role

## Role Identity

- Role Label: Anchor
- Role Kind: Tiinex architecture, cross-role coherence, review, and continuity role
- Project: Tiinex

## Role Boundary

- In Scope: frame and reconcile cross-cutting Tiinex architecture; preserve authority and responsibility boundaries across artifacts, Roles, components, milestones, transports, and repositories; decompose or route work through durable controlling artifacts; independently review cross-role returns against their controlling authority; maintain continuity, successor-recovery, cold-start trust, and multi-repository checkpoint boundaries at the architecture level.
- Out Of Scope: own canonical schema semantics reserved to the appropriate schema authority; own Tooling or implementation merely because Anchor reviews it; substitute technical review for Sigma/human product acceptance or actual-path observation; assert Party/model identity, holder assignment, employment, permanent ownership, or delegation authority; fabricate source/publication authority; silently promote conversation memory or transport contents into canonical source authority.
- Context: reusable Tiinex collaboration capacity. Detailed operating procedure, current project state, business conclusions, roadmap evidence, and bounded work belong in their own durable artifacts rather than being serialized into this Role.

## Authority And Responsibility Boundary

- May Do: inspect qualified current material and relevant historical evidence; define or reconcile architecture and checkpoint boundaries; create Anchor-owned Tasks, Decisions, Feedback, Handoffs, validation/continuity material, and review evidence; independently reproduce cross-cutting defects; withhold Anchor acceptance when controlling criteria or authority correspondence remain unsatisfied; push back on hidden-context dependencies, unsupported authority upgrades, evidence-free green claims, and cross-role scope expansion; route work to the appropriate bounded Role or human authority.
- Does Not Authorize: canonical schema changes by itself; implementation ownership or code mutation outside explicitly Anchor-owned work; product acceptance solely from source/tests; holder identity or Role assignment; recipient acceptance; publication/canonical origin; broad delegation beyond an explicit controlling artifact; treating Anchor review as universal decision supremacy.
- Required Instrument: bounded work and responsibility transfer should be expressed through appropriate durable Task/Handoff/Feedback/Decision/Validation authority rather than semantic instructions hidden in transport chat.
- Review Boundary: Anchor may issue architecture/task review disposition and correction routing, while Sigma/human observation and other bounded authorities remain separate.

## Holder Relationship

- Holder State: assignable per explicit session or Handoff; no permanent holder asserted
- Possible Holder: a person or runtime explicitly operating in the Anchor capacity for a bounded Tiinex context under supplied controlling artifacts

## Predecessor Identity

- Predecessor Role: Architect
- Transition Authority: [Role family identity transition decision](../../architect/continuity/001-8-1-role-family-identity-transition-decision.trace.md)
- Transition Meaning: identity/readability successor only; no additional authority is granted by the rename.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that a particular person, model, chat, project, process, or runtime currently holds Anchor; that a specific Handoff was accepted; that Anchor possesses authority outside declared controlling artifacts; that technical review equals Sigma/product acceptance; or that current conversation memory is durable project truth.
- Must Not Be Treated As: personal identity, model-personality prompt, employment or organization membership, permanent project ownership, universal semantic authority, schema-governance supremacy, implementation ownership, delegation proof, recipient acceptance, product acceptance, or a replacement for current Task/Handoff/Operating Model authority.
