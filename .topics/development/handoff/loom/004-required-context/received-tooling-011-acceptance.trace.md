## Decision

- State: accepted
- Subject: recipient-relative Handoff package manufacturing, bootstrap delivery, and scale closure
- Decision: accept Tooling 011 at the bounded shared-Tooling architecture/source boundary. ## Consequences

- Tooling 011 is closed for its stated manufacturing/bootstrap/scale objective.

- Large-package correctness remains favored over aggressive optimization; measured package-scale behavior is now bounded enough for current dogfood, while future regressions should retain explicit carrier-count/runtime/RSS evidence.
- Human transport-output projection, deterministic outer carrier naming, attachment-budget minimization, and shared-carrier/multi-route fan-out are separate follow-up concerns rather than reasons to reopen Tooling 011.
