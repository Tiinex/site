- The fresh recipient identifies the package-local `001` root and uses the visible READ/orientation surface before broad arbitrary archive archaeology when the host makes the flat package visible.

- The recipient recognizes package-local Parent continuity as navigable lineage and does not treat every generated child artifact as an independent root.
- The recipient resolves and grounds the carried current Loom Role before substantive interpretation of the transferred work.
- The recipient identifies this Task as the controlling work and correctly understands that the task is read-only cold-start qualification, not a request to resume carrier implementation or mutate source.
- The recipient preserves the distinction between package-local carrier nodes and the exact durable Workspace source representation inside the Workspace archive.
- The recipient reports any native archive/filesystem archaeology, fallback, unresolved material, or ambiguity rather than hiding it; eventual recovery after unnecessary broad archaeology is not preferred-path PASS.
