- business-write-and-cross-lane-integration
  - Retained By: Anchor
  - Responsibility: Master Anchor remains the only Business writer and owns cross-lane reconciliation, final Business disposition and Full Recovery integration.
  - Boundary: secondary Anchor returns qualified results; it does not update Business directly.

- site-playthings-lane
  - Retained By: Anchor
  - Responsibility: secondary Anchor owns bounded orchestration of the delegated Site/Playthings readiness lane, including specialist fan-out, owner-routed blockers, technical qualification and precise human-gate preparation.
  - Boundary: ownership is orchestration responsibility, not blanket source-mutation authority across carried Workspaces.

  - Retained By: Sigma

- no-business-mutation
  - Kind: excluded-scope
  - Description: secondary Anchor must not mutate Business; Business follow-up is returned to Master Anchor.
  - Responsible Party Or Role: secondary Anchor.

- no-silent-sibling-mutation
  - Kind: excluded-scope
  - Description: carried Playthings/App/Core context does not itself authorize source mutation in those sibling Workspaces.
  - Responsible Party Or Role: secondary Anchor.

- no-feature-expansion
  - Kind: excluded-scope
  - Description: keep Verse Playthings Major 003 on technical/viewability readiness and Sigma-test preparation; do not widen into new product features before readiness is established.
  - Responsible Party Or Role: secondary Anchor.

- real-browser-human-gate
  - Kind: unresolved-dependency
  - Description: if the existing Site real-browser command remains the qualified next human gate, present Sigma the exact command/result contract only after the technical prerequisites are genuinely ready; do not fabricate PASS.
  - Responsible Party Or Role: secondary Anchor / Sigma.

## Completion Expectation

- Signal Kind: return
- Signal Meaning: return qualified Site/Playthings lane state, exact blockers/delegations, technical browser-readiness result or deterministic Sigma gate, and enough recovery/checkpoint context for Master Anchor to integrate the lane without reconstructing the secondary session.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Mean: secondary Anchor has Business write authority, every carried Workspace is mutable, the current Site human gate has already passed, or Playthings has Sigma product acceptance.
- Must Not Be Used To Claim: source, participant, process, product-acceptance or cross-repository authority beyond exact qualified declarations.
