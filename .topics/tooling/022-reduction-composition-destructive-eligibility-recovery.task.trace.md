# Major 011 — Reduction Composition + Destructive Eligibility/Recovery

## Objective

Make Tiinex Reduction composable and recoverable enough that ordinary semantic narrowing, reduction-of-reductions, and any later destructive lineage collapse share one truthful provenance model without turning Reduction itself into completion or deletion authority.

The Major must preserve two distinct layers. First, `tiinex.reduction.v1` remains the domain-neutral observable carry-forward event and may itself reduce prior qualified Reductions when the source/loss/recoverability boundaries remain explicit. Second, physical disappearance of current semantic material remains a separate fail-closed destructive-lineage eligibility decision bound to exact pre-delete bytes, exact source snapshots, exact candidate delete set, complete disappearing-leaf coverage, and immutable Parent closure to the truthful surviving boundary.

The current shared lifecycle/readiness projection from Major 010 may provide qualified currentness/blocker/re-test facts where semantically relevant, but it must not be replaced by the older reduction-preflight lexical lifecycle token heuristic and must not become destructive authority by itself.

## Planning Context

- Segment: Reduction composition, destructive eligibility, and recovery proof.
- Purpose: make hierarchical Reduction and cross-repository recovery deterministic while preserving ordinary Reduction versus destructive eligibility as separate authority surfaces.
- End Condition: qualified canonical Reduction/destructive-eligibility semantics plus one shared adapter-neutral Tooling path that can project hierarchical Reduction inputs, prove or block exact destructive-lineage eligibility on intact material, and demonstrate immutable recovery/expansion without performing broad historical deletion.
- Expected Handoffs: approximately 4–7, reforecast after Axiom reconciles the older Reduction safety decision against current Docs and Major 010 lifecycle semantics.
- Confidence: medium. A strong prior Axiom safety decision and a planning-only shared preflight already exist, but canonical Docs follow-through, reduction-of-reductions composition, exact snapshot/delete-set receipt binding, cross-repository immutable closure, and recovery proof remain incomplete.
- After This Segment: Viewer/schema parity with selective Playthings evidence remains later; Pages/deployment/release, Foundation exit, and broad schema scaling remain later still.

## Done Criteria

- Axiom reconciles current `tiinex.reduction.v1`, the accepted `010-1-1` Reduction-before-delete decision, current Validation/Relation/Parent authority, and Major 010 lifecycle/readiness semantics; it decides the smallest canonical follow-through, including whether the previously required conditional destructive-lineage validation surface belongs directly in Reduction or an explicitly owned adjacent maintained contract.
- Ordinary Reduction qualification remains distinct from destructive-lineage eligibility. A valid Reduction artifact alone never authorizes deletion.
- Reduction-of-reductions is explicitly supported as a domain-neutral composition pattern: a Reduction may use prior qualified Reductions as source material while preserving source identity, carry-forward state, loss/uncertainty, validation, and recoverability to fuller immutable material.
- Reduction scope is independent of physical materialization location. Repository/workspace placement must not manufacture ancestry, collapse boundaries, completion, or ownership semantics.
- The shared destructive eligibility result binds exact Reduction bytes, exact intact source snapshot identities for every repository used by the proof, and the exact candidate destructive set. Any change invalidates/requires requalification.
- Complete disappearing semantic leaf coverage is derived from the intact graph plus the planned destructive set rather than self-authorized by prose. Ambiguous semantic/transport/fixture classification fails closed.
- Every disappearing leaf has immutable repository+commit+path recovery identity and complete declared Parent closure through disappearing intermediates to the nearest truthful surviving semantic boundary; cross-repository Parent traversal remains first-class.
- Active, unresolved, disputed, unaccepted, fixture-required, or otherwise operative branches fail destructive eligibility unless their obligations are truthfully retained/reissued under qualified current material.
- The existing planning-only `reduction-preflight` lexical `lifecycleStatus/currentStatus` token heuristic is reconciled. Generic lifecycle truth must come from the shared Major 010 normalized lifecycle/currentness facts or another explicitly qualified semantic input; ad hoc strings remain bounded observations and may not decide destructive eligibility.
- Loom provides one shared adapter-neutral Reduction composition/eligibility/recovery path for CLI, LLM, Viewer, and VS Code. Presentation layers may not fork destructive policy.
- Neutral regressions cover at least: ordinary non-destructive Reduction; Reduction-of-Reductions; known loss; unresolved loss; exact candidate-set receipt mismatch; changed Reduction bytes; semantic/fixture ambiguity; uncovered deleted semantic artifact; current operative branch; local Parent closure; cross-repository closure; missing immutable source; current placement parent distinct from historical closure endpoint; recovery expansion from hierarchical Reduction; and lifecycle/currentness authority mismatch.
- A concrete domain-neutral hierarchy fixture demonstrates `event material -> daily Reduction -> monthly Reduction of daily Reductions -> yearly Reduction of monthly Reductions`. Financial/donation material may be one fixture, but no PayPal-specific or financial-domain meaning enters core Reduction semantics.
- Shared Tooling can demonstrate recovery/expansion from the reduced current view back to the immutable source lineage and can re-audit a hypothetical/applied changeset boundary. Broad destructive apply/delete remains separately gated and is not automatically opened by preflight success.
- Anchor independently reconciles specialist returns and qualification. Public process/orientation is updated only if current first-contact material would otherwise misrepresent Reduction versus lifecycle/readiness/destructive authority.

## Scope

- Canonical Reduction composition and destructive-lineage eligibility semantics required to complete the prior accepted safety direction.
- Reduction-of-reductions and recoverable hierarchical carry-forward.
- Cross-repository immutable Parent-closure proof and source snapshot identity.
- Shared planning/qualification/recovery Tooling on intact material.
- Reconciliation of the older Reduction preflight lifecycle heuristic with the shared lifecycle/readiness projection.
- Neutral multi-workspace fixtures and exact failure-mode receipts.

### Explicit exclusions

- No broad historical deletion or repo-scale cleanup merely because eligibility can be calculated.
- No remote destructive mutation by Anchor/Axiom/Loom.
- No inference that a qualified Reduction proves Task completion, re-test PASS, closure, or release readiness.
- No Viewer parity/Playthings merge or transfer of Playthings-specific concepts.
- No Pages/deployment/release repair.
- No Foundation exit claim or broad schema catalog/fanout work.
- No Windows-specific acceptance-host requirement.
