# Playthings

## Project Identity

- Description: Playthings is the Site-owned experimental/product-development branch for spatial, generative, and lived-lineage Viewer experiences built on Tiinex Viewer semantics.
- Boundary: Playthings is a subproject of Tiinex Viewer. It may experiment with presentation and source-production methods, but it does not redefine canonical artifact, Parent, schema, Role, Workspace, or Business authority.

## Project Purpose And Scope

- Description: Develop Playthings-specific Viewer experiences and their supporting visual-source, runtime, world, presentation, and exporter work while keeping every durable development lineage traceable through Viewer back to the Tiinex organization root.
- Boundary: Playthings-specific work is authored in the Site workspace/branch for this track. Business may carry domain-neutral process guidance, but this track does not add Playthings-specific artifacts to the Business main-refactor workspace.

## Parties And Resources

- Relevant Parties: Sigma for explicit human feedback/acceptance where required; Anchor for bounded architecture, review, and continuity; specialist roles when explicitly handed work.
- Relevant Resources: the current Tiinex/site Playthings workspace, the carried Tiinex/business Viewer project and Roles, canonical Docs schemas, and frozen Playthings visual/runtime evidence owned by Site.

## Coordination State

- Description: The historical Playthings Viewer lineage and current Carrier Major work are Site-owned continuations. This root now provides their explicit organizational bridge to the current Business Viewer project.
- Boundary: Changing this root does not rewrite the historical meaning of child work. Existing descendants retain their task/evidence/handoff semantics; only the missing organizational root continuity is corrected.

## Milestones And Outcomes

- Description: Near-term outcomes include a recoverable visual-source production process, robust derived asset/export paths, and continued Viewer/Playthings iteration without orphaning Site lineages from organizational context.
- Boundary: Process stabilization does not freeze the larger planned schema/artifact sheet taxonomy, world/interior/detail requirements, grid choices, fullscreen presentation, time model, or future Playthings scope.

## Interpretation Limits

- Does Not Prove: that Playthings is production-ready, accepted as the whole Viewer product, or authorized to change Business main-refactor work merely because this cross-workspace project root exists
- Must Not Be Treated As: a new Tiinex organizational root, a replacement for Tiinex Viewer, a merge of the Playthings and main-refactor branches, or acceptance of any particular visual/runtime implementation
