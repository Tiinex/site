## Manual Test Frontier

- Configure the desired ZIP/local workspace material in ordinary Viewer columns and enter Playthings globally.
- At Origin/fresh replay, confirm one continuous earth is visible with terrain only: no repo islands, decorative roads, pre-existing castle, or artifact icon field.
- Watch an ordinary root/Task-like lineage: the Plaything should appear and move smoothly through a real animation sequence, not pop between discrete screenshots; a Task interaction may disappear afterward without leaving a permanent world icon.
- Watch an Organization event: the relevant structure must not pre-exist; the Plaything should reach its generated nearby scene and the place should visibly build/materialize before it remains in the world.
- If nested Organizations are present, confirm morphology may vary by consecutive Organization depth while all remain in the same earth.
- Watch a sibling branch: the original living leaf should remain while the new branch Plaything visibly forks and can travel through the actual historical branch point before continuing to its deterministic nearest-free destination.
- Watch a supported Handoff with an already-built Organization place: a receiving Plaything should be able to emerge from nearby Organization context and run to the Handoff scene rather than a portal/road simply appearing.
- Confirm no roads are painted merely because two lineage nodes exist. If future road logic is added, it must be motivated by observed movement/history rather than graph decoration.
- Pause during the middle of a walk/action and resume; the same active sequence should continue instead of restarting or teleporting.
- Drag the timeline to earlier/later event boundaries, then Play; world state should reconstruct deterministically and movement should begin from that boundary.
- Re-open/replay the same unchanged material and confirm generated placements, scan choices, body variants, and shirt colors remain stable rather than randomizing.
- Add only suffix/delta material and confirm prior generated geography does not reshuffle before the new events play.
- Hover a living leaf to inspect Tesseract lineage history without permanently cluttering the world with every ancestor.
- Reach Now and confirm Playthings become completely still; no walking/action loop continues without an active observed event.
- Judge perceived game feel specifically: continuity of motion, acceleration/arrival, walking cadence, interaction liveliness, branch clarity, and whether world growth now reads as caused rather than as a slideshow.
