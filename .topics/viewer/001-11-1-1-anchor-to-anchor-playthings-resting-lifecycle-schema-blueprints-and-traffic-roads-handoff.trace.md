## Manual Test Frontier

- Replay ordinary history without manual timeline scrubbing and watch populations around the 4-day and >7-day idle thresholds. Long-idle must not dim/flicker the entire sprite population; leaves entering resting state should walk concurrently toward already-built habitats instead of disappearing.
- Pause during a multi-leaf resting migration. All migrants should freeze in-place with the active event; resume should continue the same routes.
- Watch settlement occupancy counters while migration occurs. Count must not jump before the migrants arrive; after completion the leaves may be compressed into the settlement count.
- Find a resting leaf at a playhead before a later castle/workshop is built. It must not disappear into that future structure.
- Replay/inspect .schema.md artifacts for Organization or Workspace schemas. They must appear as blueprint scenes/Tech Tree material only and must not create castles, workshops, habitats, or living Playthings.
- Hover persistent places and compare clearances. A castle should reserve visibly more navigation/placement space than a Plaything; actors/other places should no longer treat its center point as its only occupied area.
- Let repeated resting/other travel converge on a settlement. After repeated completed use of the same corridor, look for a faint trail, then stronger path/road as thresholds are crossed. No road should exist before actual traffic causes it.
- Observe future movement near an established road. Routes may detour onto it when the discounted travel cost is cheaper, but should still take a shorter direct route when road detour provides no benefit.
- Rewind before the artifact event that first crossed a trail/path/road threshold. The future road upgrade must not be visible at the earlier playhead.
- Inspect the Tech Tree at normal display scale. Titles/schema/status/descriptions/actions should be materially easier to read; horizontal scrolling is acceptable and preferable to tiny text. Implemented only remains ON by default.
- Retest role hats, schema blueprint echoes, Fit World, Follow, Handoff package reconciliation, hotbar/transition interactions, and Viewer responsiveness to ensure no regression from the prior checkpoint.
