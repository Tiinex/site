# Playthings original pixel art and world readability

## Objective

Use the stream-preview artwork only as a visual quality benchmark for silhouette, clustered shading, material contrast, depth, and world-object presence, while keeping the Tiinex Playthings expression original and compatible with the one-earth history-caused-world design. Replace the remaining debug-rectangle feel with a stronger but still inexpensive pixel language, spend most extra SVG detail on rare landmarks rather than population-wide decoration, align navigation footprints with the richer visible structures, make traffic-worn roads read as worn earth rather than debug lines, and increase Tech Tree legibility again rather than shrinking the whole graph to fit.

## Done Criteria

- Living Playthings use a stronger original pixel silhouette with readable head/body/boots/trousers, individual shirt variation, small deterministic hair/body variation, and role hat livery retained as the stable role indicator.
- The same resolved role continues to produce the same role-hat shape and color; art polish must not introduce role drift or semantic meaning into body/hair/shirt variation.
- Repeated living sprites remain deliberately cheap enough that visual polish does not undo the Viewer/Playthings performance gains from 001-9/001-10.
- Organization castle/fort/village artwork gains clustered highlights/shadows, battlements/windows/doors, ground/path dressing, and stronger silhouette while remaining an original Tiinex presentation rather than copying external reference art.
- Workspace place artwork gains readable workshop materials, roof depth, windows, door/crate/chimney details, ground/path dressing, and cluster composition while remaining history-built and presentation-only.
- Neutral terrain can include deterministic grass, stone, bush, and sparse flower marks without becoming semantic geography or pre-seeded historical structures.
- Synthetic trail/path/road rendering uses layered earth-wear strokes with increasing visual strength; road visibility and thresholds remain entirely caused by completed observed traffic.
- Organization and Workspace navigation footprints are resized to contain the richer visible silhouette, including tall castle towers/flag area, so A* and placement do not route through newly visible landmark pixels.
- Existing sparse A*, traffic wear thresholds, resting lifecycle, schema-blueprint hard boundary, package reconciliation, Viewer performance caches, Follow/Fit, hotbar/Create authority, and one-earth semantics remain unchanged.
- Tech Tree cards/titles/schema/status/body/actions become larger again; horizontal scrolling remains preferable to micro-font fitting.
- Implemented-only remains enabled by default and real schema Parent topology remains the only Tech Tree topology.
- Focused Playthings tests, TypeScript, architecture, browser-import, UI-shape, smoke, integration, and static-regression-aware validation remain green with zero introduced static debt.
