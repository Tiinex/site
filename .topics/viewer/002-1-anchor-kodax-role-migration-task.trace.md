# Kodax Role Migration For Viewer Implementation

## Objective

Materialize Kodax as a durable Tiinex Role before the Viewer Navigation Parity implementation is delegated, preserving the supplied Implementation Engineer responsibilities, exclusions, and return-handoff expectation while making peer-role boundaries explicit.

## Done Criteria

- A schema-valid `tiinex.party.role.v1` Kodax artifact exists and is self-integrity qualified.
- The role preserves frontend/backend implementation, integration testing, code quality, refactoring, and performance optimization as Kodax responsibilities.
- The role explicitly excludes autonomous product decisions outside requirements, final product acceptance, unauthorized remote mutation, and scope expansion.
- The role requires a self-contained return carrier that states what changed, how it was validated, known risks, and the exact next action.
- Kodax is distinguished from Loom shared Tooling/mechanics ownership, Sigma human acceptance, and Anchor progression/disposition authority.
- No permanent holder, model identity, repository authority, or remote-write permission is inferred by the role artifact.

## Scope

Role migration only. Do not begin Viewer implementation, merge `playthings`, change shared Tooling semantics, publish, release, push, or claim product acceptance as part of this task.

## Dependencies

- [Viewer Navigation Parity Recovery — Active Major](002-anchor-viewer-navigation-parity-recovery-active-major-task.trace.md)
- Current `tiinex.party.role.v1` schema and portable authoring path.
- Supplied Kodax Implementation Engineer role card; all durable role semantics required for future use must be represented in the resulting Role artifact rather than relying on the card remaining available.
