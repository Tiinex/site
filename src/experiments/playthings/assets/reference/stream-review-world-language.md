This is a much more convincing Playthings checkpoint.

The important change is not just that there are more pixels on the screen. The experiment is beginning to behave like an actual multiverse rather than a diagram of artifacts.

We now have two distinct realms visible at once: Tiinex Business and Tiinex Site. They have different visual identities, their inhabitants are grouped inside their own worlds, and there is a visible connection between them rather than everything being flattened into one enormous graph.

At the top, Playthings is reporting two realms, one hundred and nine living lineages, two hundred and nine observed artifacts, and three world gates. That terminology is starting to matter because it changes how the same Tiinex information is presented without changing what the information means underneath.

The key conceptual improvement is that the little figures are no longer supposed to represent random files.

A living inhabitant represents the current head of a lineage. The historical material behind that lineage becomes its memory and trail through the world. That means a long-lived thread of work can become something that appears to have a history rather than merely appearing as a pile of disconnected documents.

And the larger structures are beginning to serve as landmarks.

So instead of saying, here is artifact number forty-two connected to artifact number forty-three, Playthings can eventually say, in visual terms, this lineage belongs around this part of the world, it came through this history, and it connects to that other realm through this relationship.

That is much closer to the fantasy-world idea we were aiming for.

There is still plenty to critique.

The realms are visually much denser now, especially Site. At a glance, Site looks almost like a crowded city while Business feels smaller and calmer. That may eventually become a useful reflection of the underlying repositories, but it also means the design will need to make sure density remains readable rather than becoming visual noise.

The creatures are also still closer to abstract pixel inhabitants than fully developed characters. We have moved well beyond the original debugging icons, but we have not yet reached the point where every world feels like a place with a strong personality of its own.

And that is fine for this stage.

The major question for this pass was whether multiple loaded repositories could become genuinely separate Verses and still preserve their real relationships.

Visually, we now have evidence that they can.

The dotted connection between Business and Site is especially important. That is not supposed to be decorative scenery. A gate between worlds should exist because Tiinex resolved a real cross-repository relationship that justifies it.

If there is no relationship underneath, Playthings should not invent a pretty bridge.

And if you look at the lower status line, the experiment is still being unusually explicit about uncertainty. There are unresolved lineage findings being preserved rather than quietly discarded. That means the fantasy layer is still respecting cases where Tiinex does not have a complete answer.

The world is also sitting at Now and still.

So the temporal rule has survived this much larger visualization. Nothing is bouncing around just because we happen to be watching it. If we refresh and Tiinex discovers genuinely new material, that new delta should be what receives motion. When the world catches up with the present again, it should settle.

That is one of the design choices I like most about this experiment because it gives animation meaning.

Movement becomes information.

Stillness becomes information too.

There is also a new Playthings follow-up visible back in the normal Tiinex Feed. So the work that produced this world is itself becoming part of the Tiinex lineage that Playthings may eventually visualize.

Which creates a slightly amusing recursive situation.

Tiinex is now recording the history of building Playthings, while Playthings is being built to visualize the history recorded by Tiinex.

We are not quite at tiny creatures watching us build tiny creatures yet.

But we are getting dangerously close. :P
