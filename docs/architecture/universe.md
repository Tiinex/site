# Universe

Universe is the root entry verse for Tiinex Site.

It is the first arrangement a reader sees. Its job is to present the first Multiverse without making the reader choose a projection before they can work.

## Human-first role

- Start with workspaces, not explanations.
- Show column workspaces as the default continuity surface.
- Keep each workspace source boundary visible and separate.
- Let Feed, Tree, and future Verse selectors live inside a workspace pane.
- Do not change source truth by arranging panes.

## Relationship

- Universe contains the first Multiverse.
- Column Verse is the first Multiverse implementation.
- A Multiverse may contain another Multiverse if it expands into a new material context.
- Cycles/roundtrips are blocked by expansion identity, not by arbitrary nesting depth.

## Renderer boundary

Universe and Verse are Tiinex concepts. Leaflet, SVG, Canvas, D3, WebGL, DOM cards, and CSS columns are renderers.
